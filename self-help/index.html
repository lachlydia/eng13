Your goal

I can understand and use the 15 chunks from Text 4 – in a comment, in a discussion and in the Abitur.

How to practise (about 20 minutes)

  1. 1Flashcards: learn the chunks. Mark what you know.
  2. 2Match: English chunk → German meaning.
  3. 3Gap fill: use the chunks in new sentences.
  4. 4Type it: German → English, from memory.
  5. 5Use it: write your own sentences, e.g. for your comment.

Do the exercises again on another day. Repeating after 2–3 days helps most.
